The Business Problem

Why Traditional Accounts Payable Is Broken

Finance departments process millions of invoices yearly using error-prone manual data entry or naive LLM wrappers that hallucinate math and fail regulatory audits.

⏱️

Manual Data Entry & Backlogs

AP specialists spend $12 to $15 per invoice manually re-typing line items. Turnaround cycles take 7 to 14 days, creating month-end bottlenecks and missed vendor early-payment discounts.

🧮

LLM Arithmetic Hallucinations

Raw Generative AI models struggle with financial precision: they hallucinate totals, miscalculate quantity × price, and silently produce rounding errors that violate general ledger accounting principles.

🌍

Cross-Border Tax Non-Compliance

Managing distinct tax regimes—US State/County Sales Tax, UK VAT (20%), German MwSt (19%/7%), and Spanish IVA (21%/10%)—leads to costly tax compliance violations and rejected foreign deductions.

🛡️

Duplicate Fraud & Audit Vulnerability

3.6% of invoices contain duplicate submissions or altered totals. Without 7-year legally binding immutable records (WORM) and tamper-proof event logs, companies risk failing SOX and IRS compliance audits.

The ApexAP Solution

Deterministic AI + Pure Rule Enforcement

We separate extraction from validation: LLMs read unstructured documents, while pure Python code executes strict financial arithmetic and compliance checks.

01 • Multi-Modal Routing

PyMuPDF Text Density

Automatically routes searchable PDFs to fast GPT-4o-mini and raster scans to GPT-4o Vision, cutting extraction token costs by 68%.

02 • Zero-Tolerance Math

±$0.02 Python Validation

Deterministic code checks quantity × price = line total, subtotal + tax = total, and cross-checks against country tax brackets.

03 • Tri-Tier Router

90% Straight-Through Flow

≥90% confidence + balanced math auto-approves. Discrepancies route to AP Specialists; invoices > $10,000 route to Finance Managers.

04 • Regulatory Sealing

7-Year Immutable WORM

Inbound documents sealed in Azure Blob with a 2,557-day immutable retention policy and SHA-256 duplicate fingerprinting.
